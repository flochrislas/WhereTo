Top tier ramen in Shibuya.
Very great ramen, strong taste, good price. Place average for a ramen restaurant, not very confortable (narrow counter, crappy pop music...), but they do have a couple of tables for groups. They speciallized in tonkostsu ramen. Spicy miso available as well, and even cold soupless ramen in summer. You can ask for up to 2 free kaedama (fair amount of additional noodles), and choose for the soft/hardness of the noodles.
